Rawa

What is Rawa?

Rawa is a personal financial management tool for individuals in Aotearoa. Rawa makes your financial data transparent and easy to understand by tracking your income against expenses, manage multiple accounts over different finance providers, and helping you achieve your financial goals.

Rawa is currently under active development. A macOS and iOS version will released in late 2026. If you have any questions, please get in touch using the following email: angus@qgt.co.nz.
